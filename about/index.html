About Land Scout

Land Scout helps you find land for sale in Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur starting from the money you have. You tell it your budget and what kind of place you want, and it shows the plots that fit.

Where the listings come from

Every few hours we read the public land listings on nepalhomes.com and gharghaderi.com. Each plot links back to its original listing, and you contact the seller there. We don't edit listings or accept payment to rank them.

How we measure a plot

  • Size and price come from the listing. We convert sizes to ropani-aana-paisa-dam and work out the price per aana. If a price looks wrong (for example “Rs 1 per aana”), we say so instead of guessing.
  • Distance to the main road, shops, schools, clinics and green areas come from OpenStreetMap. When the seller pinned the plot on a map, these are measured from the pin. Otherwise they're measured from the middle of the locality and marked “rough”.
  • Near the city means within 5 km of Kathmandu, Patan or Bhaktapur Durbar Square. The edge of the city is 5 to 10 km, and the outskirts are farther than 10 km.
  • “What your money buys” uses the median asking price per aana of current listings. Asking prices are usually higher than final sale prices.

What we are not

We are not a broker, we don't check ownership papers, and we can't promise a listing is still available. Always follow the checks before you buy.

Contact

Open a report on our GitHub page (a free GitHub account is needed). If a listing is wrong or yours and you want it removed, tell us the listing number shown on the card, for example #10138.
