Before you pay an advance

  1. See the original Lalpurja and match the seller's citizenship to it.
  2. Match the Kitta Naksa to the plot, and confirm ownership at the Malpot office.
  3. Check there is no loan or court case on the land (rokka), and that the road is public.
  4. Pay through a bank, with a written agreement.
